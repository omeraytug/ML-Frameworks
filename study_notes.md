# Glossary

- **Dot Product**: the dot product is an algebraic operation that takes two equal-length sequences of numbers (usually coordinate vectors), and returns a single number - np.dpt(x, y)

- **L2 Norm**: The L2 norm, or Euclidean norm measures the straight-line distance from the origin to a point in vector space, calculated as the square root of the sum of the squared vector components - np.linalg.norm(x)

- **Cosine Similarity**: Cosine similarity measures the cosine of the angle between two non-zero vectors, determining how similar they are in direction regardless of their magnitude - dot(a, b) / (norm(a) \* norm(b))
